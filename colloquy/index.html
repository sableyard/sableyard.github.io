Privacy you can check.

Colloquy has no accounts and no servers, so there is nothing of yours for Sableyard to keep, sell or lose.

What Colloquy never does

  • Create an account for you
  • Show ads
  • Run analytics or trackers
  • Upload your chats to Sableyard

What it does

  • Keeps your chats on your phone
  • Encrypts API keys with the Android Keystore
  • Sends messages only to the provider you choose
  • Lets you delete all local data from Settings

One honest note: the provider you pick does receive your messages, under its own privacy policy. Colloquy sends them nowhere else. Read the full privacy policy.

Free. Bring your keys.

Colloquy costs nothing. You pay your AI provider directly for what you use, and some of them offer free tiers.

Questions

Does Colloquy collect my data?

No. There is no account, no analytics and no ads. Your conversations stay on your phone.

Which AI providers work?

OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, xAI, Mistral, Groq, DeepSeek, Perplexity and OpenRouter, plus custom compatible endpoints.

Do I need my own API keys?

Yes. You add a key from each provider you want to use and pay that provider directly for what you use. Some providers offer free tiers; check theirs.

Are my keys safe?

Keys are encrypted with the Android Keystore and are only sent to the provider they belong to.

Does it work offline?

You can read saved chats offline. Sending a message needs an internet connection.

Which Android versions are supported?

Android 8.0 or newer.

How do I install it?

Download the APK, allow installs from your browser when Android asks, and open the file. Android may show a warning because Colloquy is a new app outside the Play Store.

Is Colloquy free?

Yes, Colloquy is free to use today. You only pay your AI providers for what you use. If that ever changes, it will be announced here.
